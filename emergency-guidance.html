Emergency guidance

Know the signs of a heart attack and what to do in the first minutes.

Suspect a heart attack? Act within minutes.

Do not wait to see if it passes. Do not drive yourself to hospital.

  1. Call your local emergency number now
  2. Stop all activity and sit down
  3. Chew an aspirin if advised and not allergic
  4. If unresponsive, start CPR and use an AED

Warning signs

Chest pain or pressure

Tightness, squeezing or heaviness.

Shortness of breath

With or without chest discomfort.

Spreading pain

Arm, jaw, neck or back.

Cold sweat or nausea

With light-headedness.
