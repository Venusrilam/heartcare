Hypertension care

Personalised plans to bring your blood pressure under control.

Why it matters

High blood pressure often has no symptoms, but over time it strains the heart and arteries. Regular checks and a steady plan lower the risk of heart attack and stroke.

Who should visit?

  • Readings that are often above normal
  • A family history of high blood pressure
  • Diabetes or kidney problems
  • Anyone already on blood pressure medicine who wants a review

What to expect

  • Careful measurementRepeat readings, and a home monitoring plan if needed.
  • Lifestyle reviewSalt, food, activity, weight and sleep.
  • TreatmentMedicines only when needed, explained clearly.
  • Regular follow-upReviews to check progress and adjust the plan.
