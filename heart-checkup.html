Heart check-up

A complete review of your heart health in a single visit.

What is it?

A routine check of the main factors that affect your heart: blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar and weight. Your doctor combines the results to estimate your risk and explain what to do next.

Who should get one?

  • Adults over 30
  • Anyone with a family history of heart disease
  • People with diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol
  • Smokers and people with low activity levels

What to expect

  • ConsultationTalk through your history and symptoms.
  • Basic testsBlood pressure, blood tests and an ECG if needed.
  • Results explainedYour doctor explains every number in plain words.
  • Written planClear next steps for diet, activity and follow-up.
