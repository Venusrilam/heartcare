Heart diseases

Understand common heart conditions, their warning signs and when to get help.

Common conditions

Coronary artery disease

Arteries that feed the heart narrow because of plaque, reducing blood flow and raising heart attack risk.

Heart attack

A blocked artery cuts blood supply to part of the heart. It needs emergency care.

Heart failure

The heart cannot pump enough blood for the body's needs. It can be managed with treatment.

Arrhythmia

An irregular, too-fast or too-slow heartbeat.

High blood pressure

Often has no symptoms, but strains the heart and arteries over time.

Valve disease

One or more heart valves do not open or close properly.

Know what your heart is telling you

Chest pain or pressure

Tightness, squeezing or heaviness.

Shortness of breath

During light activity or at rest.

Spreading pain

Arm, jaw, neck or back.

Cold sweat or nausea

With light-headedness.

Unusual fatigue

Feeling worn out for no clear reason.

Irregular heartbeat

Racing, fluttering or skipped beats.

Suspect a heart attack? Act within minutes.

Educational content only, not a substitute for medical advice.

  1. Call your local emergency number now
  2. Stop all activity and sit down
  3. Chew an aspirin if advised and not allergic
  4. If unresponsive, start CPR and use an AED
