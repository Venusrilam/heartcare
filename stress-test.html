Stress test (TMT)

See how your heart responds when it has to work harder.

What is it?

A treadmill test (TMT) records your heart rhythm and blood pressure while you walk at increasing speed and slope. It can show problems that do not appear when you are resting.

Who may need it?

  • Chest discomfort or breathlessness during activity
  • People with several heart risk factors
  • Checking fitness after treatment, as advised by your doctor

What to expect

  • BeforeWear comfortable clothes and walking shoes. Ask us whether to take your usual medicines that day.
  • DuringYou walk on a treadmill while the team watches your ECG and blood pressure.
  • DurationAround 30 to 45 minutes in total.
  • AfterYour doctor explains the result and next steps.
