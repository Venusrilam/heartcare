Cardiac rehab

Guided exercise and diet to recover safely after a heart event.

What is it?

A supervised programme that helps you regain strength and confidence after a heart problem. It combines safe exercise, healthy eating, stress management and education about your medicines.

Who is it for?

  • After a heart attack
  • After a stent or bypass surgery
  • People living with heart failure
  • Anyone your doctor recommends it for

What to expect

  • AssessmentYour doctor checks your heart and fitness first.
  • Personal planExercise matched to your condition and goals.
  • Regular sessionsSupervised activity with your heart rate monitored.
  • Progress reviewsWe adjust your plan as you get stronger.
