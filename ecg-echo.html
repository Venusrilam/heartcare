ECG and echocardiogram

Painless tests that show how your heart beats and pumps.

What are they?

An ECG records the electrical activity of your heart using small stickers on your chest. An echocardiogram uses sound waves to create a moving picture of your heart's chambers, valves and pumping strength.

Who may need them?

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Palpitations or an irregular heartbeat
  • Shortness of breath or swelling in the legs
  • A heart murmur or a family history of heart disease

What to expect

  • PreparationUsually none. Wear a loose top.
  • ECGA few minutes lying down with stickers on your chest.
  • EchoA gel and a handheld probe on your chest, about 20 to 30 minutes.
  • ResultsYour doctor explains what the pictures show.
